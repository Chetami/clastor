## 1. What Clastor Is (one-liner)

**Clastor is the all-in-one management platform that runs the business side of tutoring — scheduling, students, and billing — so tutors can focus on teaching.**

It replaces the spreadsheet + calendar + paper-invoice stack most solo and small-team tutors run today, and replaces it with one place where a lesson scheduled, a student messaged, a payment chased, and an invoice sent all live together.

### The 15-second pitch

> You became a tutor to teach. But most of your week disappears into juggling a calendar app, a student spreadsheet, a notebook of invoices, and a inbox full of "are we still on this Thursday?" messages. Clastor puts all of that in one place — schedule a lesson and the calendar invite, reminder, and invoice line item are handled for you. You teach. We run the rest of the business.

## 2. The Problem We Solve

Independent tutors are excellent teachers and reluctant administrators. Today they run their business from a patchwork of tools never designed to work together:

- **A personal calendar** for scheduling — manually copying lesson times, no link to students or money.
- **A spreadsheet** for tracking students, subjects, rates, and who owes what.
- **Manual invoices** — typed up per lesson, sent by hand, chased by hand.
- **DMs and texts** for reminders, reschedules, and "did you get my payment?"
- **No clear picture of income** — how much did I actually earn this month? Next month?

The result: hours lost every week to admin, missed payments, double-bookings, forgotten reminders, and a vague anxiety about whether the business is actually healthy. Clastor collapses that patchwork into one system where everything is connected.

### Why now

- The tutoring market is large, growing, and increasingly made up of independent and online tutors (post-2020 shift to remote learning stuck).
- Existing tools are either (a) generic scheduling apps with no concept of students/lessons/billing, or (b) heavyweight agency software built for tutoring companies, not individuals.
- Solo tutors have been quietly assembling their own workflows — and quietly hating them.

## 3. Who It's For (target audience)

### Primary: Independent / solo tutors
- 5–40 regular students.
- Running it as a real business (income they depend on), not a side hobby.
- Teaching online, in-person, or a mix.
- Comfortable with modern web apps, tired of stitching tools together.
- Subjects: academic (math, science, languages, music, test prep), but subject-agnostic.

### Secondary: Small tutoring teams / partnerships
- 2–5 tutors sharing students or branding.
- Need individual logins and a shared view.

### Who Clastor is **not** for
- Large tutoring agencies/schools with complex rostering and payroll (we're not enterprise software).
- Casual one-off tutors with a single student (overkill for now — they can use a free calendar).

### Persona snapshot — "Maya, the math tutor"
Maya teaches 22 students across GCSE and A-level math, a mix of Zoom and home visits. She's organized about teaching but drowning in admin: her Google Calendar has the lessons, a Notion table has the students and rates, and she hand-writes invoices in a Google Doc each month. She's lost track of who's paid, double-booked twice this term, and genuinely doesn't know her real monthly income. She wants one tool that handles the *business* so she can get back to the *teaching*.

## 4. Value Proposition

### Headline value prop
**Spend less time running your tutoring business. Clastor handles the scheduling, reminders, and invoicing — connected, automatically.**

### Supporting value pillars (the "why it's better")

1. **One connected system, not a patchwork.**
   Schedule a lesson and it's on the calendar, the student is notified, a reminder is queued, and the hours flow straight into an invoice. No copy-paste between four apps.

2. **Students always know what's happening.**
   Automated calendar invites, reminders, and one-tap RSVP (accept/decline) mean fewer "are we still on?" messages and fewer no-shows.

3. **Get paid without the chase.**
   Turn unpaid lessons into professional invoices in a click, accept payment online via Stripe, and always know exactly who owes what — no spreadsheet archaeology.

4. **Know your business at a glance.**
   A dashboard that shows expected vs. actual income, hours taught, and what needs your attention — so you can plan instead of guess.

5. **Looks professional, because you are.**
   Branded emails, polished PDF invoices, and a public tutor profile. Clastor makes a solo operation feel like a real, trustworthy business to every student and parent.

### The "Jobs to be Done"
- *When a tutor has a new student*, they want to add them once and never re-enter their details — so scheduling and billing "just know" about them.
- *When a lesson is booked*, they want the student reminded and the time blocked — without manual follow-up.
- *When the month ends*, they want to send invoices and collect payment without a spreadsheet and a pile of DMs.
- *When a parent asks "how much do we owe?"*, they want one accurate number, instantly.

## 6. Tone of Voice

- **Confident, not boastful.** We know the problem and we solve it. No exclamation marks screaming "amazing!!".
- **Warm and human.** We're talking to a person running a small business, not a "user." Plain language, short sentences.
- **Concrete, not abstract.** "Turn unpaid lessons into an invoice in one click" beats "streamline your billing workflow."
- **Respectful of their time.** Tutors are busy. Copy should be skimmable and get to the point.
- **Empathetic to the grind.** Acknowledge that the admin side is genuinely annoying — we've been there, that's why we built this.

### Words we use
manage, connected, automatically, professional, in one place, schedule, invoice, get paid, focus on teaching, run your business.

### Words we avoid
synergy, leverage, revolutionize, cutting-edge, robust, seamless (overused), enterprise-grade.

## 7. Core Features — Framed for Marketing

The raw feature list (full inventory in the app) reframed as **benefits a tutor cares about**. Group these for the Features / Solutions section of the site.

### Scheduling that runs itself
- **Drag-and-drop calendar** — see your whole week, book one-off or recurring lessons in seconds.
- **Recurring series** — set weekly/biweekly lessons once; Clastor handles the rest, and you can edit a single session without breaking the series.
- **Two-way Google Calendar sync** — lessons appear on your Google Calendar automatically, and stay in sync if anything changes (including self-healing if an event gets deleted).
- **Working hours** — define when you're available; Clastor warns you about out-of-hours bookings.
- **Google Meet links** auto-generated for online lessons.

### Students, all in one place
- **Student profiles** — contact details, subjects, rates, notes, and linked parents/guardians.
- **Always know who owes what** — live debt tracking pulls straight from unpaid invoices.
- **Per-student timezones** — essential for online tutors with students abroad.

### Communications students actually read
- **Automatic calendar invites** (.ics) attached to every lesson email.
- **Reminders on a schedule** — configurable lead time, with anti-spam cooldown so you never nag.
- **One-tap RSVP** — students Accept/Decline straight from the email.
- **Branded, professional emails** with replies routed back to your inbox.

### Billing & payments without the spreadsheet
- **Invoices in one click** — turn unpaid lessons into a polished, itemized invoice.
- **Professional PDF invoices** with sequential numbering and your branding.
- **Get paid online** — Stripe checkout + Stripe Connect so money lands in your account.
- **Multi-currency** for international tutors.
- **Email invoices with the PDF attached**, then track Open → Paid.

### Know your business
- **Dashboard** — expected vs. actual income, hours taught, upcoming lessons, and what needs your attention.
- **Flexible reporting periods** — week, month, six months, year.

### Look the part
- **Public tutor profile** — a customizable page with a unique link (yourname.clastor…), templates, headline, bio, qualifications, subjects, and a contact button.
- **Publish when ready** — draft, preview, then go live.

### Attendance & follow-through
- **Attendance tracking** — present, late, absent (with/without makeup), cancellations.
- **Makeup credits** — issue and track automatically.
- **Lesson notes & to-dos** — keep what happened in each session, and what's next.

## 9. Objections & Rebuttals (FAQ seeds)

| Objection | Rebuttal |
|---|---|
| "I already have a calendar that works." | A calendar only holds times. Clastor connects the time to the student, the reminder, and the invoice — so nothing slips through. |
| "I'm not tech-savvy." | Clastor is built to be simple. Onboarding walks you through setup, and it works alongside the Google tools you already use. |
| "Is my data safe?" | Secure Firebase-based authentication, JWT session management, and you stay in control of your students' information. |
| "I only have a few students." | Clastor scales from your first student. The earlier you start, the less manual catch-up later. |
| "What about payments?" | Built-in Stripe support means students pay online and money lands in your account — no chasing. |
| "Do I have to switch from Google Calendar?" | No — Clastor syncs two-way with Google Calendar and uses Google Meet. It complements what you have. |
